Brainrot Bazaar is a fast-paced 3D Roblox brainrot tycoon where iconic meme mascots parade across a central elevated conveyor runway. Players buy mascots they can afford, carry them home on their heads, and auto-place them on shop pads to generate continuous passive income per second. Rare tiers and shiny mutations supercharge your earnings, while rival players can raid unlocked bases to steal high-value mascots. Survive periodic catastrophic tsunami waves by retreating to your base safe zone, and ascend at the Rebirth Shrine to multiply your global income forever.

## Run it

```sh
rojo build default.project.json --output game.rbxl && mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## How to play

- **Buy from the conveyor**: Approach the elevated conveyor runway and interact with the ProximityPrompt on parading mascots to buy them if you have enough cash.
- **Carry & auto-place**: Carried mascots perch on your character's head. Run toward your assigned colored shop plot (marked by your light beacon), and the mascot will automatically place onto any free pad when you arrive.
- **Earn passive cash**: Each placed mascot pays passive cash per second directly into your wallet. Each shop features six display pads.
- **Rarities & mutations**: Mascots span Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Mythic rarities. Random Gold (2x), Diamond (3x), and Rainbow (5x) mutations dramatically boost income rates.
- **Base security lock**: Toggle your shop sign ProximityPrompt to lock your base for 60 seconds, protecting your collection from thieves.
- **Steal from rivals**: If another player's base is unlocked, you can trigger the Steal prompt on their display pads to snatch their mascot and run it back to your own shop.
- **Tsunami hazard**: When the tsunami warning broadcast sounds, you have 10 seconds to get back inside your base safe zone. Anyone caught outside loses their carried mascot and 10% of their cash.
- **Rebirth progression**: Once you accumulate $25,000, visit the Rebirth Shrine to exchange your cash and placed collection for a permanent +0.5 income multiplier step per rebirth.
